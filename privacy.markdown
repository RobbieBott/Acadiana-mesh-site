_Last updated: November 17, 2026_

Acadiana Mesh is a volunteer community project. We don't sell ads, we don't sell or rent your information, and we only collect what we need to run the network tools and keep you informed. This page explains what we collect and what we do with it.

## This website

- **No cookies or tracking.** acadianamesh.com doesn't set cookies or use analytics or advertising trackers.
- **Hosting.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical details such as your IP address when you visit. See [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Fonts.** The site loads its font from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google when a page loads. See [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Live data.** The Reports pages load live network data from our analyzer (analyzer.acadianamesh.com).

## Newsletter sign-up

When you subscribe, we collect:

- your **email address** (required)
- your **name** (optional)
- your **phone number** (optional)

This is stored in our own newsletter system on our own server and is used only to send you Acadiana Mesh news and updates. Emails are delivered through Brevo, an email delivery service. You'll get a confirmation email first, and every newsletter has an unsubscribe link. We never sell or share your information with anyone else.

## Analyzer accounts

If you create an account on [analyzer.acadianamesh.com]({{ site.analyzer_url }}), we store your email address and your account login. Your email is used for account emails (such as confirming your account or resetting your password) and for any node alerts you choose to turn on. These emails are also delivered through Brevo.

## Mesh network data

Acadiana Mesh is a radio network, and anything sent over it can be received by anyone in range. Community observers — radios that listen to the mesh — report what they hear to our servers, and our analyzer and Live Map display it publicly. This can include:

- node names, public keys and node types
- locations that nodes share in their adverts
- signal and routing information
- messages on the Public channel and other channels whose keys are publicly known, such as hashtag channels

Direct messages and channels with private keys are encrypted, and we can't read them. Network data is also shared with neighboring community mesh networks in Louisiana. See the [Privacy tab of our Wiki](/wiki/#privacy) for tips on protecting your privacy on the mesh, such as setting an approximate location.

## Discord

Our Discord server is run on Discord's platform and is covered by [Discord's privacy policy](https://discord.com/privacy).

## Your choices

- **Unsubscribe** from the newsletter at any time with the link in any email.
- **Ask us** to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to help with information about your node shown on our tools. Email us at [{{ site.email }}](mailto:{{ site.email }}).

## Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
