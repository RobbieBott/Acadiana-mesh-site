Online = heard by the mesh in the last 24 hours · Stale = last heard 1–3 days ago · Offline = more than 3 days · Not heard = our observers haven't picked it up. Owners can mark a repeater Maintenance, Retired or Waiting for install.

“Last heard” comes live from the analyzer. Own a repeater that's missing or out of date? Sign in to manage it, or ask on Discord.

Reserved Prefixes

Repeaters are told apart on the mesh by the first 1, 2 or 3 bytes of their public key, depending on their path hash size. Pick a prefix nobody else uses before you generate your repeater's key, then reserve it with an admin.
